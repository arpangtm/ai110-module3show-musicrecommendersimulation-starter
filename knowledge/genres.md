# Genre Guide

One section per genre in the catalog. The keywords list the different ways
people actually say the genre name ("rap" instead of "hip hop", "lo-fi"
instead of "lofi") so the retriever can map loose wording onto the catalog.
Genre hints are treated as explicit requests, so they beat situation hints.

## Pop
keywords: pop, pop music, top 40, mainstream, radio hits, catchy
hints: genre=pop
Pop is built around catchy hooks and polished production. Usually upbeat and positive, mid to high energy.

## Indie pop
keywords: indie pop, indie, bedroom pop, alt pop
hints: genre=indie pop
Indie pop keeps the hooks of pop but with a looser, more homemade sound. Bright, often a bit more acoustic than mainstream pop.

## Lofi
keywords: lofi, lo-fi, lo fi, chillhop, lofi beats, chill beats, beats to study to
hints: genre=lofi
Lofi hip hop is slow, warm, and slightly dusty sounding on purpose. Mostly instrumental and made for background listening.

## Rock
keywords: rock, rock music, guitar rock, hard rock, alt rock, alternative
hints: genre=rock
Rock is guitar driven and usually energetic. Hard rock leans loud and intense.

## Metal
keywords: metal, heavy metal, metalcore, thrash, headbang
hints: genre=metal
Metal is the most aggressive genre in the catalog: very high energy, fast tempos, distorted guitars, and low valence.

## Ambient
keywords: ambient, atmospheric, drone, soundscape, space music
hints: genre=ambient
Ambient music is about texture rather than rhythm. Very low energy and good for sleep, meditation, or background focus.

## Jazz
keywords: jazz, smooth jazz, jazzy, bebop, saxophone, sax
hints: genre=jazz
Jazz in this catalog is the relaxed cafe kind: acoustic, warm, and mid-low energy.

## Synthwave
keywords: synthwave, retrowave, 80s, eighties, retro synth, outrun, synth
hints: genre=synthwave
Synthwave borrows the sound of 1980s synthesizers and film scores. Moody, steady, and great at night.

## Hip hop
keywords: hip hop, hiphop, hip-hop, rap, rapper, trap, boom bap
hints: genre=hip hop
Hip hop is rhythm and vocal driven, built around a strong beat. Mid tempo (85 to 100 bpm is common) with lots of attitude.

## EDM
keywords: edm, electronic, electronic dance, house, techno, dubstep, rave, festival, trance, drops
hints: genre=edm
EDM is electronic dance music made for big rooms. The highest danceability in the catalog, very high energy, almost no acoustic sound.

## Classical
keywords: classical, orchestra, orchestral, piano, symphony, sonata, instrumental classical, composer
hints: genre=classical
Classical tracks here are quiet piano and string pieces: very acoustic, very low energy.

## Country
keywords: country, country music, americana, honky tonk, twang, cowboy
hints: genre=country
Country leans on storytelling and acoustic guitars. Mid energy and often nostalgic.

## R&B
keywords: r&b, rnb, r and b, rhythm and blues, soul, neo soul, slow jams
hints: genre=r&b
R&B is smooth and groove based with a focus on vocals. Often romantic, mid energy.

## Folk
keywords: folk, acoustic folk, singer songwriter, singer-songwriter, unplugged, campfire
hints: genre=folk
Folk is stripped back and acoustic, usually guitar and voice. Quiet, reflective, often melancholy.

## Reggae
keywords: reggae, dancehall, ska, dub, island music, caribbean
hints: genre=reggae
Reggae has an offbeat, laid back groove. Positive and relaxed, mid energy.

## Latin
keywords: latin, latino, reggaeton, salsa, bachata, cumbia, spanish music
hints: genre=latin
Latin covers a lot, but the catalog track is a romantic, danceable mid-tempo song.

## Blues
keywords: blues, delta blues, blues guitar, bluesy
hints: genre=blues
Blues is slow, soulful, and built on expressing sadness. Low valence, mid-low energy, fairly acoustic.
