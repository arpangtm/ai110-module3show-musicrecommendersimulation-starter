# Listening Situations

Notes on what people usually want to hear in common situations. The
retriever splits this file on the `##` headings, so each section is one
document. The `hints:` line is what gets turned into preferences; the
paragraph under it is the reasoning the explanation quotes back to the user.

Hint keys: genre, mood, energy (0-1), likes_acoustic (true/false),
tempo_bpm, valence (0-1).

## Studying and deep focus
keywords: study, studying, homework, exam, focus, concentrate, concentration, reading, coding, programming, work session, deep work
hints: genre=lofi; mood=focused; energy=0.35; likes_acoustic=true
Focus music works best when it stays out of the way. Steady low-to-mid tempo, no big drops, mostly instrumental and warm sounding. Lofi is the classic choice because the beat keeps you moving without grabbing your attention.

## Working out and the gym
keywords: workout, gym, lifting, run, running, cardio, training, exercise, pump up, pumped, sprint
hints: mood=euphoric; energy=0.93; likes_acoustic=false; tempo_bpm=130
Workout music needs high energy and a strong, fast pulse, usually 120 to 150 bpm. Heavily produced tracks with punchy drums do better here than acoustic ones.

## Party and dancing
keywords: party, dance, dancing, club, pregame, celebration, celebrate, friday night, birthday
hints: mood=euphoric; energy=0.88; valence=0.8; likes_acoustic=false
Party songs are high energy, positive, and danceable. People want something they can move to, so bright, produced tracks rank above slow or sad ones.

## Sleep and winding down
keywords: sleep, sleeping, bedtime, fall asleep, wind down, insomnia, nap, night time relax
hints: mood=calm; energy=0.18; likes_acoustic=true; tempo_bpm=62
For sleep the goal is the lowest energy possible. Slow tempo, soft dynamics, and acoustic or ambient textures. Anything with a strong beat works against you.

## Rainy day
keywords: rain, rainy, gloomy, grey day, overcast, cozy, stay inside, thunderstorm
hints: mood=melancholy; energy=0.35; likes_acoustic=true
Rainy days pair well with slower, reflective songs. A little sadness is fine, and acoustic instruments make it feel cozy instead of heavy.

## Heartbreak and sadness
keywords: heartbreak, breakup, broke up, sad, crying, lonely, miss someone, heartbroken, down, depressed
hints: mood=melancholy; energy=0.32; valence=0.3; likes_acoustic=true
When someone is sad they often want music that matches the feeling rather than fights it. Low valence, slower songs, usually acoustic. If someone sounds like they are really struggling, music is not a substitute for talking to a person they trust.

## Date night and romance
keywords: date, date night, romantic, romance, dinner, anniversary, love, valentine, slow dance
hints: mood=romantic; energy=0.55; valence=0.72
Romantic music sits in the middle on energy. Smooth grooves, warm vocals, and a positive but relaxed feel. R&B and latin ballads are common picks.

## Chilling out
keywords: chill, chill out, chilling, relax, relaxing, unwind, laid back, lazy day, hang out, hanging out
hints: mood=chill; energy=0.4
Chill music is low pressure: mid-low energy, smooth, nothing jarring. It overlaps with focus music but does not have to be instrumental.

## Road trip
keywords: road trip, roadtrip, driving, drive, highway, car ride, travel
hints: mood=happy; energy=0.72; valence=0.78
Road trip music is upbeat and singable, with steady mid-to-high energy that keeps everyone awake without wearing them out.

## Late night drive
keywords: late night, midnight, night drive, city lights, 2am, neon
hints: genre=synthwave; mood=moody; energy=0.7
Late night driving music has a darker, moody feel with a steady pulse. Synthwave and atmospheric electronic music fit that city-at-night vibe.

## Morning coffee
keywords: morning, coffee, breakfast, wake up, sunday morning, brunch, cafe
hints: mood=relaxed; energy=0.4; likes_acoustic=true; valence=0.7
Morning music eases you into the day. Relaxed, warm, positive, and often acoustic. Jazz and soft folk are common cafe choices.

## Meditation and yoga
keywords: meditate, meditation, yoga, mindfulness, breathe, breathing, stretch, stretching, spa
hints: mood=calm; energy=0.2; likes_acoustic=true; tempo_bpm=64
Meditation music has almost no rhythm pushing you along. Ambient and classical textures with very low energy work best.

## Letting off steam
keywords: angry, mad, rage, furious, vent, venting, frustrated, scream
hints: mood=angry; energy=0.95; likes_acoustic=false
When people are angry they often want loud, aggressive music to burn it off. Metal and hard rock have the high energy and intensity that matches that.

## Summer and the beach
keywords: summer, beach, sunny, sunshine, vacation, pool, island, tropical, bbq, cookout
hints: mood=relaxed; energy=0.6; valence=0.85
Summer music is bright and easygoing. High positivity, mid energy, and a laid back groove. Reggae and latin tracks are a natural fit.

## Feeling confident
keywords: confident, confidence, swagger, boss, hype myself, getting ready, walk in
hints: mood=confident; energy=0.78; likes_acoustic=false
Confidence music has a strong beat and attitude. Hip hop is the usual pick, with mid-high energy and a groove that makes you walk differently.

## Nostalgia
keywords: nostalgic, nostalgia, memories, hometown, old times, throwback, remember
hints: mood=nostalgic; energy=0.5; likes_acoustic=true
Nostalgic songs tend to be mid tempo and warm, often with acoustic guitars and storytelling lyrics. Country and folk lean into this a lot.
